Who am I

Photographer & videographer.

I’m Bruna — a freelance photographer and videographer working with small, independent brands who want their social content to feel considered rather than churned out. I organise, style and shoot, from concept through to final edit, and I’ve spent the last few years learning exactly what makes a brand’s feed feel like it belongs to them.

Previously a studio technician, I now work full-time on set and on location for fashion and lifestyle brands.
